Impact

Help you can actually follow.

50% of creator fees support children battling cancer. A verified charitable partner. A public ledger for every gift.

The ribbon

Gold already means childhood cancer awareness.

We are not inventing a cause to decorate a token. The gold ribbon is a symbol families already know. HopeCoin’s job is to treat it with enough seriousness that a donation record can sit next to a meme — and both still make sense.

The path

Fees become gifts. Gifts become a line in the ledger.

01

Creator fees

Trading $HOPE generates creator fees. 50% of those fees are directed to children’s cancer support.

02

Verified partner

A charitable organization that works with children battling cancer receives the gift. One partner. A name you can read.

03

Public record

Every gift has a date, an amount, a destination, and proof. If it cannot be listed here, it is not part of the story.

Public ledger

Every gift, in one place.

Records appear here as donations are made. This is the source of truth for HopeCoin’s charity work.

Awaiting first record

The ledger is live. The first line will be written when the first gift is made.

Date, amount, partner, proof — nothing decorative, nothing missing.
