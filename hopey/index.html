Hopey

He wears the ribbon so we remember why.

A Shiba Inu. A gold ribbon. Courage, kindness, and hope — and a face for children fighting harder battles than a chart.

The face

Familiar on purpose.
Serious about the ribbon.

Shiba Inu is already part of crypto’s visual language. Hopey uses that familiarity, then changes the ending. The gold ribbon is the international mark of childhood cancer awareness. We did not invent it. We chose to wear it.

Courage
Showing up when the fight is long.
Kindness
A culture that can joke without turning cruel.
Hope
Leaving something better than a ticker.

For the community

Draw him. Meme him.
Keep the ribbon.

Hopey is the shared face of the coin. Art, stickers, edits, comics — all of it. The one request: keep the gold ribbon. That is the part that is not a joke.
